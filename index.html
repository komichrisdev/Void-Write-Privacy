Privacy Policy for Void Write

Last updated: April 16, 2026

Void Write is developed by komichris.

Void Write is designed to collect as little information as possible. The app does not require an account and does not collect your name, email address, journal entries, writing content, contacts, photos, precise location, or files.

Analytics

Void Write uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand basic app usage and improve the app. Firebase Analytics may collect basic information such as app opens, sessions, device model, operating system, approximate geography, app updates, and an app-instance identifier.

This information is used only to understand app performance and usage. It is not used to identify you personally, and Void Write does not sell user data.

Crash Reports

Void Write uses Firebase Crashlytics to help find and fix crashes. Crash reports may include basic device information, app version, operating system version, crash details, and diagnostics. Crash reports do not include your journal entries or writing content.

Advertising

Void Write shows Google AdMob banner ads. AdMob may collect and use device identifiers, including the Android Advertising ID when available, to serve ads, measure ad performance, limit ad frequency, and prevent fraud. Void Write requests non-personalized ads and does not share writing content with AdMob.

Data Sharing

Void Write does not sell or share your personal writing content. Basic analytics data may be processed by Google Firebase as part of the analytics service. Crash report data may be processed by Firebase Crashlytics. Advertising data may be processed by Google AdMob.

Data Storage and Retention

Your writing stays on your device unless you choose to delete it, back it up, or remove the app. Analytics, crash report, and advertising data are retained according to Google Firebase and Google AdMob settings and policies.

Security

Void Write uses standard platform tools and services to help protect app data. No method of electronic storage or transmission is perfect, but the app is designed to avoid collecting unnecessary personal data.

Children

Void Write is not directed to children under 13.

Contact

Changes
